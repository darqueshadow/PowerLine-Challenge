# Handoff: Egg Timer Alien Sheets (approved as they are)

Andrew has approved the six alien sheets in Egg Timer's image folder as they are. Gemini got worse with every retry, so there will be no more art rounds. Work around what's here, and use the fallback (whole-body squash, stretch and bob) wherever a puppet rig doesn't work.

Andrew's earlier ruling to flag problems rather than patch them no longer applies to these six. Fix what you can in code, and only come back if a sheet is unusable.

## Sheet by sheet

**Crab (cute):** usable as a puppet. Two legs came joined at the knee (the L-shapes near the bottom); split them there. There are a couple of extra leg pieces; use the best 8.

**Scuttler (horror):** usable as a puppet. The body still has one stubby leg on its right side and small claws underneath. Place the loose legs so they cover those. The fangs on its face stay as mouthparts; the separate curved pincers are the mandibles that open and close. Ignore the loose eggshell chips and small motion marks.

**Octopus (cute):** the body still has its tentacles attached. Suggested workaround: treat the body with its attached tentacles as one whole-body piece on the fallback wobble, and add a few loose tentacles (built from the segment pieces on the sheet) around it, so something still curls. Your call on what looks best.

**Grabber (horror):** the same problem and workaround as the octopus. The body sits in eggshell halves and a slime puddle; cut those away if you can, since the game already draws the shell halves at the nest. The loose tentacle segments and purple drips are usable.

**Worm (cute):** Gemini added spider claws; the worm has no legs, so ignore them. Use the head, body segments, tail and antenna.

**Wriggler (horror):** usable as planned (body on the fallback wobble, glowing insides on their own layer, feelers animated). Ignore the extras Gemini added: the Scuttler head in the top left and the purple drum-shaped pieces.

## Check before building

- Make sure the octopus and Grabber sheets in the folder are the **first-run** versions: the octopus sheet has the yellow-eyed octopus in the middle, and the Grabber sheet has the tentacled mass sitting in eggshell. Later retries put a crab and a Scuttler in those files by mistake. If you find the wrong one, stop and tell Andrew.
- Everything else follows the Hatch Rulings: wave rules, scurry-only for cute aliens, and a hatch of about 2 seconds.
