The real-world flow this copies
Hospital call: the crew gets a 10-minute VS. When it's up, the SSC removes it and adds an STR CAV. The STR runs its 10 minutes, then the SSC removes it.
Refusal (no transport): the crew gets a 10-minute VS, and that's it.
Roughly 70% of calls are hospital calls.
Changes
1. One game mode

Remove the three current CAV options/modes. The only CAV that evolves is the VS, and only sometimes. Everything below is the single mode.

2. Every egg starts as a VS

Every egg is laid with a VS CAV.

3. About 70% are hospital eggs
When the egg is laid, the Queen drops a "Hospital" marker on it. That's how the player knows it's a transport.
The other ~30% are refusal eggs, with no marker.
Make the 70% a setting.
4. When the VS runs out

The egg starts to hatch, and the player has the same hatch window as now on every egg.

Refusal egg: the player RCAVs it. Done, as now.
Hospital egg: the player must RCAV the VS and then CAV STR it, both within that same hatch window. RCAV alone isn't enough; the egg keeps hatching until the STR is added.
5. Adding the STR

When the player CAVs STR on a hospital egg:

The Queen's (Mom's) head pops in, she repairs the egg, looks at the player and giggles.
The egg is whole again, and the STR's time starts.
When the STR runs out, the egg starts hatching again with the normal window, and the player RCAVs it as normal.
