Egg Timer: egg-laying sound (Chat ruling, 2026-09-25)

When an egg is laid on the cord:
- Squeeze: a short wet, rubbery squelch while the bulge travels the last stretch
  of the cord.
- Pop: a cartoon "finger out of the mouth" pop the moment the egg drops into the
  nest.
Synthesize both in code for now (no files); they can be swapped for recorded
sounds later.

- Random small pitch variation on each lay, so repeats don't sound identical.
- Mix them clearly under THONG, the error buzz and the hiss; never mask a cue.
- If several eggs are laid at once, cap overlapping pops (e.g. max 2 at a time).
- Mute covers them. Pause and background-tab behaviour: same as other sound effects.
Normal rig/commit/push rule. Bring design calls back to Chat as E-numbered items.
