Andrew's rulings on the three follow-ups from the splat art build.

## Locked

1. **Stage trigger: keep speed-based (E26 stands).** A faster clear gives a neater splat, a slower one a grosser splat, exactly as built. Nothing to change. This closes the would-be E45 question. The earlier handoff wrongly listed the trigger as undecided.
2. **Keep key features clear of shell fragments.**
   - Stage 3: the antenna, including its tip.
   - Stages 4 and 5: the eyes and face.
   - Stages 1 and 2: no extra restriction beyond staying inside the splat outline.
   All existing fragment rules still apply: random pick, shrink, turn and flip, 3–4 pieces on stage 1 climbing to 6–8 on stage 5, and nothing outside the outline.
3. **Flying shell pieces use the new shell sprites.** The pieces that fly across the board and get hosed into the trough should use the ten new shell sprites instead of pieces cut from the egg picture, so the shell looks the same everywhere.

## Unchanged
