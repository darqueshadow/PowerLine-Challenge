## Proposal accepted

Your wave proposal is accepted as written:

- **Wave 1:** every hatch is cute.
- **Wave 2:** the first hatch is always horror. After that, the chance of horror grows through the wave (about 25% a quarter of the way in, 75% three quarters in).
- **Wave 3 onward:** horror only, with a random scurry or lunge as now.
- **Which alien:** from a shuffle bag, so the same one doesn't come out twice in a row.
- Every choice is a setting in config.js.

This replaces the old "the escapee is always horrific" ruling.

3. **Hatch length:** about 2 seconds instead of 1.4, so the animation has time to show. Tune it once the aliens move. The Game Over wait on the last hatch grows to match.
