# Handoff: Egg Timer Hatched Aliens (EGG-TIMER-HATCH)

This picks up the deferred item EGG-TIMER-HATCH: what comes out when an egg hatches because the player was too slow. This is for the Egg Timer game only; the Rec-Bay 4 table doesn't change.

**This step is a report and a proposal. Don't build anything yet.**

## The art

Two concept sheets from Gemini are in Egg Timer's image folder. Identify them by what's in them:

1. **Cute set:** three smiling purple aliens. A round spider-crab with eyes on stalks, an octopus with big yellow eyes, and a worm with one antenna.
2. **Horror set:** three creepy versions of the same idea.
   - **The Scuttler:** a hunched spider on long jointed legs, with a cluster of red eyes and mandibles.
   - **The Grabber:** a dripping mass of tentacles with slit-pupil eyes growing all over it.
   - **The Wriggler:** a see-through grub with glowing green and purple insides and a head of purple feelers.

These are concepts. Final game art gets made to your specs (step 3 below). Known issues to fix at that point: the outlines are thinner than the game's thick dark plum outline, and the Scuttler's mandibles still look slightly like clasped hands.

## Andrew's ruling: which aliens hatch

- **Level 1:** cute only.
- **Level 2:** horror starts appearing alongside cute.
- **Level 3 onward:** horror only.

Recommended by Chat, not yet ruled (propose, and Andrew confirms):

- **The first horror hatch on level 2 is guaranteed,** not left to chance, so every player gets that moment.
- **Level 2 ramps up:** mostly cute early in the level, mostly horror by the end.

## Animation: legs and tentacles should wiggle

Andrew wants the aliens animated, especially the legs and tentacles. The preferred approach is **puppet-style**: each alien built from separate pieces (body, each leg, each tentacle), with the game swinging and curling each piece on its own. The fallback, for any alien that doesn't split up well, is wobbling the whole picture (squash, stretch, bob, sway). Frame-by-frame drawings are out: Gemini can't keep a creature consistent across frames.

## What to report back

1. **The hatch moment now:** what the game currently shows when an egg hatches, how long it lasts, and where it happens (in the nest, climbing out, running off the board).
2. **Level length:** roughly how long levels 1 and 2 last, so Andrew knows how quickly players will meet the horror set.
3. **Animation fit:** whether puppet-style animation works in the game as it's built, and what it would take.
4. **The art format you need for final art:** size, and for puppet-style, which parts to separate for each alien and how (for example, one body picture plus a sheet of loose legs or tentacles, like the shell pieces). Chat will turn this into Gemini prompts.
5. **A proposal for the ramp** on level 2, using the recommendations above.
